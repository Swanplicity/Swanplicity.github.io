How collecting works

The path work and money actually take.

  1. This miner hashes on your device, working jobs the pool hands out.
  2. Swan Bridge carries the work to the pool and carries the pool’s jobs back.
  3. When a whole block is found, the pool pays the payout address directly.
  4. The money lands in your GoldenSwan Wallet. The bridge never touches the wallet or any money — it is a relay, nothing more.

Terms of Service

The full terms for GoldHurricane Miner. When a value-verified Owner kit opens this app, the Owner’s acceptance of these terms is recorded automatically — there is no gate and nothing to tap.

1. What this app is

GoldHurricane Miner is a personal browser-based Bitcoin miner by Casey Swan / Swanplicity. It runs entirely in a web page on the owner’s own device, performs real double SHA-256 hashing in Web Workers, and speaks the Stratum mining protocol to a mining pool through the owner’s own Swan Bridge software.

2. Personal owner copy

This copy is built for one owner, Casey Swan, and carries that owner’s connection kit: the bridge address, the pool and backup pool, the worker name, and the payout wallet address. It is not a public service, not a shared mining platform, and not offered to anyone else.

3. How mining and pools work

The miner links through the bridge to the pool, subscribes, authorizes with the payout address joined to the worker name, receives jobs, hashes them, and submits any result that meets the pool’s difficulty target. The pool alone decides whether a submitted share is accepted or rejected. Share counts shown in this app move only when the pool replies.

4. No earnings guarantee

Nothing in this app promises, estimates, or implies earnings of any amount. Hashrate shown is a measurement of local work only. Past hashing, accepted shares, or time spent mining do not create any right to payment. Earnings may be zero for a very long time.

5. Solo / lottery pool truth

Both pools named in the kit are solo, lottery-style pools. Under solo mining, nothing is paid for shares alone. Payment happens only if a complete valid block is found, and the block reward goes to the payout address. A phone or browser is millions of times slower than real mining rigs, so at browser hashing speeds finding a block may effectively never happen.

6. Electricity, heat, and battery costs are the owner’s

Mining uses real electricity, produces real heat, and drains a battery much faster than normal use. All energy costs, battery wear, heat, and any device wear from running this app are the owner’s responsibility. Higher worker counts increase all of these.

7. Pool rules

10. The bridge’s role
