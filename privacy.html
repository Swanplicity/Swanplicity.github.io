Privacy Policy

Swanplicity — GoldenSwan Wallet and the Swanplicity miner apps
Effective November 17, 2026

Swanplicity is a product of Casey's Cinema Creations (owner: Casey Swan). This policy explains, in plain words, what information our apps use, why, and what we never do with it. Questions: caseyallenswan@gmail.com.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google," Google tells us your name and your email address so we can create your account and recognize you when you come back. That is all we receive from Google. We never see or store your Google password.

Account information in the apps

Our miner apps keep your account on your own device: your name, your email address, and your password and birthday stored only as salted one-way hashes (scrambled values that cannot be read back). Your birthday is used for one thing only: proving it is you if you ever need to reset your password. GoldenSwan Wallet keeps your account profile and your balance history so the wallet can show your money and your activity.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not give it to advertisers. We do not ask for, store, or have access to your bank login, your card number, or your Bitcoin seed phrase or private keys — those stay yours, in your hands only.

Sharing

The only outside service involved in sign-in is Google, for the sign-in itself. If the law ever required us to hand something over, that would be the only exception to this policy.

Your choices

You can use a password instead of Google sign-in wherever both are offered. To ask what we hold about you, or to have your account information deleted, email caseyallenswan@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
