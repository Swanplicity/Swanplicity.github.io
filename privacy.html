Privacy Policy

Swanplicity — GoldenSwan Wallet, the Prestige casino and game catalog, KC Swanplicity, and the Swanplicity apps
Effective November 19, 2026

Swanplicity is a product of Casey's Cinema Creations (owner: Casey Swan). This policy explains, in plain words, what information our apps use, why, and what we never do with it. Questions, access requests, and deletion requests: deshawnjohnson.talentscout@gmail.com (Deshawn Johnson, Casey's agent).

Signing in with Google

Where an app offers "Sign in with Google" (GoldenSwan Wallet and the Miner Portal), Google tells us your name and your email address so we can create your account and recognize you when you come back. That is all we receive from Google. We never see or store your Google password.

GoldenSwan Wallet

The wallet keeps your account profile and your balance and activity history so it can show your money and your activity. Your GSW Balance is a dollar balance held in the app's own records until you cash out. GoldenSwan Wallet does not collect ID documents today. If ID verification is ever added, this policy will be updated — naming the provider that checks the documents — before the first document is collected.

Miner apps and the Miner Portal

Casey's current miner apps and Miner Portal run without accounts or logins. They open and mine to the Bitcoin address entered in them, and they keep their settings on your own device. If a future edition adds accounts, this policy will describe it before that edition ships.

Prestige casino, AI Music Creator, and avatar photos

If you create a character from a photo in the Prestige casino or the AI Music Creator, your photo is sent to the Avatar Service, which uses Google Gemini to generate your character. The casino also keeps play records (balances, bets, wins, bonuses) under a Player ID so the games can run; if you choose to connect Supabase, those play records are stored in that connected database under your Player ID. We do not sell your photo or your play records.

Swan Inspire

Swan Inspire's built-in coach is scripted and runs on your device; your chats stay in the tab's memory and clear when the tab closes. If you turn on the optional Boost, the messages and any screen frames you choose to share are sent to Google Gemini using your own Gemini key, which is held only in the tab's memory. The translator uses an offline phrasebook first and may send text to MyMemory for phrases it does not know.

Prestige Games Provider Catalog

When you send a licensing inquiry from the catalog, you send us your name, company, business email, market, platform, and the order details you write. That inquiry arrives by email and is used only to answer you about licensing the games.

KC Swanplicity browser

KC Swanplicity keeps your bookmarks, folders, and settings on your own device and keeps no browsing history or logs. Searches go to the search provider you use. In the connected edition, the optional Swan AI panel sends your AI requests to Google Gemini using your own Gemini key, which is held server-side; without a key, it sends nothing.

Standalone tool apps

Our standalone tools (InvoiceQuick, QRQuick, MenuQR, RateCalc, ConcreteCalc, WeddingCalc, CoinCollect, TipBTC, LinkLeaf, MineCalc, AuditionPlug, and the rest) work on your own device: whatever you type — business details, menu content, or payment-page information — stays in your browser's local storage on your device and is not sent to us. Some tools load web resources when they open (Google Fonts, and in CoinCollect the QRCode.js library from the cdnjs network); loading a resource sends your IP address to that network, the same as loading any web page.

Payments and the Payment Portal

The Payment Portal stores only the owner's product links. When you buy something, your payment is handled by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout — your card and bank details go to Stripe, never to us.

Outside services, by job

Google — sign-in, and Gemini for avatar generation, Swan Inspire Boost, and KC Swanplicity's Swan AI (connected edition, your own key, held server-side). Stripe — payments. Supabase — optional casino play records, only if you connect it. MyMemory — Swan Inspire translation fallback. That is the whole list.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not give it to advertisers. We do not ask for, store, or have access to your bank login, or your Bitcoin seed phrase or private keys — those stay yours, in your hands only. If the law ever required us to hand something over, that would be the only exception to this policy.
