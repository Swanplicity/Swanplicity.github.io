Frequently Asked Questions

What is Swanplicity?

Swanplicity is Casey Swan's umbrella for his apps and projects, built under Casey's Cinema Creations. This site is its public home — the wallet, the miners, the bridge, and the game catalog all live here.

What is GoldenSwan Wallet?

GoldenSwan Wallet is a wallet app where Swan-to-Swan sends are instant inside the app and your Bitcoin address lives on one clear screen. Cash-out is Bitcoin-only, to your own Bitcoin address, with fees shown before you confirm.

How do the miners work?

The miners run in your browser and connect through Swan Bridge to a solo mining pool. Solo pools pay only when a whole block is found — the full block reward goes to the wallet address the miners are set to. That means most days pay nothing, and a found block pays all at once. The miners show only real, pool-reported numbers.
