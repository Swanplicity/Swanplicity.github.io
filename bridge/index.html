Swan Bridge for PC

Swanplicity

One small program. Every miner connects through it.

Swan Bridge is the relay the browser miners talk through: miners connect to it on your own PC, and it passes their work to the pool and the pool's jobs back. It holds no keys, no wallet, and no money — the pool pays the miner's Bitcoin address directly.

1. The bridge program

Download swan-bridge.js — the bridge itself. It needs Node.js on your PC.

2. The starter

Download Start-Bridge.bat — double-click it to run the bridge, and it restarts the bridge by itself if it ever stops.

  1. Put both files in one folder on your PC.
  2. Double-click Start-Bridge.bat and leave the window open — it will say miners connect at ws://127.0.0.1:3333.
  3. Open the Miner Portal and your miners connect through the bridge on their own.
